Support

LoadOut loads the bar for you, works out your maxes and logs your training. Here's help with the most common questions.

Your account

Is LoadOut free?

Yes. Your account is free and needs no payment details.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam folder. The email comes from LoadOut and contains a link that opens the app and signs you in. Still nothing after a few minutes? Write to us below.

I forgot my password

On the Log In screen, type your email and tap Forgot password?. Open the link in the email on your iPhone, and LoadOut asks you for a new password.

How do I delete my account?

In LoadOut, open Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your account is deleted right away, together with all the training stored in it and on that iPhone. This can't be undone.

Your training

Where are my workouts stored?

On your iPhone first, and in your account. LoadOut works without signal: anything you log in the gym is copied to your account once you're back online. You can see when it last synced under Settings → Account.

How do I get my workouts on a new iPhone?

Install LoadOut and log in with the same account (same email, Apple or Google sign-in). Your workouts, plans, records and gym setup download automatically.

I signed in with another account and my workouts are gone

Each iPhone shows the training of the account that is signed in. Log back in with your own account and your workouts come back from it. Changes made without a connection that hadn't synced before signing out can be lost, so sign out when you're online.
