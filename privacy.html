LoadOut

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

The short version

  • Your workouts, plans, records and gym setup are saved on your iPhone and stored in your account, so they come back on a new phone. They are used only to run the app for you.
  • For your free account we keep your email address, an account ID and your name, if you enter one or Apple or Google shares it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → your name at the top → Delete Account.

Who we are

LoadOut (on the App Store as “LoadOut Lifting”) is an iPhone app for barbell training made by Joel Schaeren, Switzerland. Joel Schaeren is responsible for the processing of your personal data described here.

Questions or requests about your data: joelschaerenvfx@gmail.com

Your training data

What you log in LoadOut is saved on your iPhone first, so the app works without a connection: workouts and sets, plans and the planner, personal records, bodyweight, custom exercises, your gyms, bars and plates, and your settings (units, goal, appearance, text size, weekly goal, reminder time, Now panel and rest timer preferences).

While you are signed in, the app copies this data to your account whenever it has a connection, so you can restore it on a new or reinstalled iPhone. It is stored with your account in the EU and is visible only to your account. We use it only to provide the app to you: we don't sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Reminders for planned workouts and the rest timer are local notifications created on your iPhone. LoadOut's widgets read the data on the same iPhone.

If you sign in to a different account on the same iPhone, the training on that iPhone is replaced by that account's. Your own stays in your account.

What we collect for your account

Email addressTo create and sign in to your account, confirm it, and reset your password. With Sign in with Apple you may choose Apple's private relay address instead.
PasswordOnly if you sign up with email. It is stored as a secure hash; nobody can read it.
NameOnly if you enter it in your profile, or Apple or Google shares it when you sign in with them. It is shown only to you.
Account IDA random identifier for your account, and the identifier Apple or Google gives us for your sign-in.
Training dataYour workouts, plans, records, bodyweight, custom exercises, gyms and settings, to keep them in step with your account and restore them on another iPhone.
Sign-in recordsWhen you signed up and last signed in, and technical data such as the IP address of sign-in requests, kept in the account service's security logs.

We use this data only to run your account (legal basis under the EU GDPR: performance of our agreement with you, Art. 6(1)(b)). We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Services we use

  • Supabase runs the account service and stores your account and training data in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, if you use it. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google handles Sign in with Google, if you use it. LoadOut asks only for your name, email address and basic profile. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Supabase also sends account emails, such as password resets.
  • Apple delivers the app through the App Store and TestFlight. If you agree to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports and anonymous usage figures with us; while you test a beta, also the feedback you choose to send. This happens under Apple's terms, and LoadOut itself adds nothing to it.

These providers process data on our behalf and only for these purposes. Some of them are based in the United States; where data leaves Switzerland or the EU, it is protected by the safeguards these providers offer, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

What we don't collect

LoadOut has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising identifier. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or the Health app, and it doesn't read other apps' data.

How we protect it

Data travels between the app and your account encrypted (HTTPS). In the account service, each row of training data is tied to your account and the database only lets that account read or change it. Passwords are stored as secure hashes.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, it is removed from the account service straight away, together with all training data stored in it, and the app clears it from your iPhone; copies in the provider's backups are overwritten after a limited time.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your account data, have it corrected or deleted, object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Delete your account yourself in the app (Settings → your name at the top → Delete Account), or write to the address above and we'll answer within 30 days.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority in your country.

Children

LoadOut is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change matters for how your data is used, we'll also tell you in the app.
