2. Your training

What you log is saved on your iPhone first, so LoadOut works without a connection:

  • workouts, with their exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, effort (RPE) and notes;
  • WODs with their parts, scores and splits, and the WODs you keep in My WODs;
  • plans and the planner, with reminder times;
  • personal records and your own exercises;
  • your gyms with their equipment, bars and plates;
  • your settings, such as units, training goal and styles, HYROX division, appearance, text size, weekly goal and timer preferences.

While you're signed in, the app copies this data to your account whenever it has a connection, so it's on your other iPhones too and comes back on a new one. It's stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany), and only your account can read it.

We use it only to provide LoadOut to you: to show your log, progress and records, to work out weights, plateaus and suggestions, and to keep your iPhones in step. Logging is up to you: enter only what you want to keep. Notes are free text and are stored like the rest of your training.

Legal basis: our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

3. Bodyweight

Bodyweight is health data, which the law protects specially (under the FADP it's sensitive personal data). LoadOut keeps it only if you agree: before your first weigh-in, the app explains what happens with it and asks for your explicit consent.

  • With your consent, your weigh-ins are saved on your iPhone and in your account like your training, and used only for your bodyweight log and trend.
  • We record with your account whether you agreed or withdrew, to which wording and when, so we can show it.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time: Settings → your name at the top → Withdraw Bodyweight Consent. Your weigh-ins are then deleted on your iPhone and in your account. Withdrawing doesn't make the processing before it unlawful.
  • If you don't agree, you can use everything else in LoadOut.

Lifts and other training entries can also say something about your fitness. They're used only as described in section 2.

Legal basis: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a); FADP Art. 6(7)).

6. What stays on your iPhone

Some things LoadOut does entirely on your iPhone. None of this reaches us:

  • Suggestions and calculations. Plateau fixes, weight increases, plate loadings and one-rep-max estimates are worked out on your iPhone by fixed rules.
  • Reminders and timers. Reminders for planned workouts, the rest, set and WOD timers and the reminder before a free trial ends are notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Live Activities and widgets read LoadOut's data on the same iPhone.
  • Import. The export file you choose from another app is read on your iPhone and isn't uploaded. The workouts and exercises found in it become part of your training (section 2).
  • Export. Export My Data creates a file on your iPhone. Where you save or send it is up to you.
  • Siri and Shortcuts can open LoadOut's tools; Apple handles what you say to Siri under its own policy.
  • Backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes LoadOut's data on it.

LoadOut doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or the Health app, has no advertising identifier, and doesn't read other apps' data.

11. No tracking, ads or AI

  • No tracking or analytics. LoadOut contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools and doesn't track you across apps or websites. Neither the app nor this website sets cookies or similar trackers, so there's nothing to consent to.
  • No marketing emails. We only send emails your account needs, such as confirming your address, resetting your password or telling you your password changed. If we ever want to send news or offers, we'll ask for your separate consent first, and every such email will have an unsubscribe link.
  • No AI. LoadOut doesn't use artificial intelligence, and no AI service receives your data. We don't use your data to train AI models and don't give it to anyone for that purpose.
  • No automated decisions or profiling. Nothing in LoadOut decides anything about you with legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR Art. 22, FADP Art. 21). Its suggestions are proposals: you decide what you lift.

12. Security

Data travels between the app and the account service encrypted (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider. The database lets each account read and change only its own data. Passwords are stored as secure hashes. On your iPhone, LoadOut's data is protected by iOS data protection, and your sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at high risk, we'll tell you without delay.

14. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data and for information about how it's processed, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Much of this you can do yourself in the app:

  • Get a copy: Settings → your name at the top → Export My Data gives you everything in your account as a JSON file.
  • Correct it: edit or delete any entry in the app, and your name in your profile.
  • Withdraw consent: Settings → your name at the top → Withdraw Bodyweight Consent.
  • Delete it: Settings → your name at the top → Delete Account.

Your right to object. Where we process your data based on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that outweigh your interests or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

For anything else, write to joelschaerenvfx@gmail.com. It's free. We answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email address, so nobody else can get your data.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority in your country.

15. Children

LoadOut isn't meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

16. Changes
