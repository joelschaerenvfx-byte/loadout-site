Your training data

What you log in LoadOut is saved on your iPhone first, so the app works without a connection: workouts and sets, WODs and their scores, plans and the planner, personal records, bodyweight, custom exercises, your gyms, bars and plates, and your settings (units, how you train, appearance, text size, weekly goal, reminder time, Now panel and rest timer preferences).

While you are signed in, the app copies this data to your account whenever it has a connection, so you can restore it on a new or reinstalled iPhone. It is stored with your account in the EU and only your account can read it. We use it only to provide the app to you: we don't sell it, use it for advertising or profiling, or share it with anyone else.

Bodyweight and training entries can say something about your health. They are optional, only you see them, and you can delete any entry, or everything, at any time.

Reminders for planned workouts and the rest timer are local notifications created on your iPhone. LoadOut's widgets read the data on the same iPhone.

  • No tracking or analytics. LoadOut contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and doesn't track you across apps or websites. How we protect it

Data travels between the app and your account encrypted (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider. In the account service, each row of training data is tied to your account and the database only lets that account read or change it. Passwords are stored as secure hashes. On your iPhone, the data is protected by iOS data protection.

Your rights

You can get a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Most of this you can do yourself in the app:

  • Get a copy: Settings → your name at the top → Export My Data gives you everything in your account as a JSON file.
  • Correct it: edit or delete any entry in the app, and your name in your profile.
  • Delete it: Settings → your name at the top → Delete Account.

For anything else, write to joelschaerenvfx@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email address, so nobody else can get your data.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority in your country.

Children

LoadOut is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date and version at the top. If a change matters for how your data is used, we'll tell you in the app and ask you to read it before you continue.
